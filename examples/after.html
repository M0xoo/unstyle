How a swap works

  1. Someone asksPriya taps her Friday shift and picks who could cover. Only people with the right role and free hours show up.
  2. A colleague says yesTom gets one message, not the whole kitchen. He accepts or ignores it.
  3. You approveYou see the change and the new hours for both of them before you tap. The rota on everyone’s phone updates.
